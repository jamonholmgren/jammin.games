The origins... of Gunship Origins

Gunship Origins takes its name from the classic helicopter combat series. The games mixed simulation, mission planning, and combat action, from the early Apache cockpit to later multi-ship flights.

Learn more...

MicroProse (1986), U.S Gold/Probe (Sega Genesis)

Amiga, Amstrad CPC, Atari ST, Commodore 64, FM Towns, MS-DOS, MSX, PC-98, X68000, ZX Spectrum, Mega Drive

Released in 1986, Gunship was an early helicopter combat-flight simulator, putting players in the AH-64 Apache for low-level combat missions. Sold together with Gunship 2000.

MicroProse (MS-DOS 1991, Amiga 1993)

MS-DOS, Amiga, PC-98, Amiga CD32, PlayStation

Gunship 2000 followed in 1991, reaching Amiga in 1993. Multiple helicopter types, multi-helicopter flights, and mission planning expanded the original game’s tactical possibilities. Sold together with Gunship.

Buy on Steam Buy on GOG

Hasbro Interactive (2000)

Windows PC

Released in 2000, Gunship! continued the series on Windows PC, combining helicopter simulation with a more accessible approach to combat action.
