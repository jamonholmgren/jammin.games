'JAMMIN'

Jamon Holmgren

Founder/Development Lead

Jamon started programming at age 12 on his family's 286 and 486 PCs, making games in QBasic, and never found a reason to stop. He has been the founder of a software development agency for over two decades and in 2024, he founded Jammin Games to build the combat helicopter game he'd wanted to make since playing Gunship 2000 as a kid.

Jamon's role on Gunship Origins is primarily leading the team, programming, and game design / vision.

When he's not coding or flying virtual helicopters, he's probably playing hockey, driving a tractor around his 3 acre property in the Pacific Northwest, or spending time with his wife, 4 kids, and his granddaughter.
