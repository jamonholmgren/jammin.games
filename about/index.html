Our Team

'DEADSLAP'

Denton Holmgren

Art Director

Denton is a developer and artist with a wide range of skills from years working with the Godot engine. In Gunship Origins, he handles the integration and tuning of the 3D artwork, map design and building out the tools we use to create the setting.

When he's not working on Gunship Origins, Denton is enjoying life in Finland with his wife and friends, and probably still thinking about better ways to optimize the poly count on tree models.

'JAMMIN'

Jamon Holmgren

Founder/Development Lead

Jamon started programming at age 12 on his family's 286 and 486 PCs, making games in QBasic, and never found a reason to stop. He has been the founder of a software development agency for over two decades and in 2024, he founded Jammin Games to build the combat helicopter game he'd wanted to make since playing Gunship 2000 as a kid.

Jamon's role on Gunship Origins is primarily leading the team, programming, and game design / vision.

When he's not coding or flying virtual helicopters, he's probably playing hockey, driving a tractor around his 3 acre property in the Pacific Northwest, or spending time with his wife, 4 kids, and his granddaughter.

𝕏 @jamonholmgren Twitch @jamonholmgren

'JONBLOOR'

Jon Heavens

Game Design

Jon is Graphic Designer & Illustrator for over 15 years, working and freelancing within various industries from Architecture, to Publishing, to Band Merchandise. A fan of flight sims from a young age, his earliest gaming memories include Gunship 2000 (Amiga 500) and Jane's Longbow 2 (PC).

He brings experience from working with various flight sims, such as the 'DCS: Kiowa Warrior Module', 'Nuclear Option' and the upcoming 'Combat Pilot'.

Jon's role on Gunship Origins is varied. From game design / vision, to UI/UX, 2D artwork, testing, community management, marketing and more.

When he's not designing heads-up displays, he's likely doing freelance work for bands, playing guitar, watching 80's 90's action/horror classics or drinking some sort of coffee whilst contemplating a return to Brazilian Jiu Jitsu.
